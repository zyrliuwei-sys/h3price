# Privacy Policy

_Last updated: September 6, 2026_

## What this site does

This is an independent H3 Max pricing reference with a cost calculator, a prompt-writing tool, and a model comparison. It is not operated by MiniMax, fal, or Google. Visiting these pages and using their public tools does not require an account.

## Public tools

The cost calculator computes estimates in your browser. The prompt tool assembles text in your browser; it does not generate video or send your prompt to a model provider. Text placed in a URL may appear in browser history and hosting logs. Following a provider link takes you to a separate service governed by that provider’s policies.

## Accounts and cookies

Email registration asks for an email address and password, plus agreement to this policy. An initial display name is derived from the email prefix and can be changed in account settings. The authentication service stores a password hash, verification status, and session records; it uses cookies to maintain your sign-in. Email verification and password resets use the configured email delivery provider. Optional third-party sign-in receives the profile information supplied by that provider. Language preferences also use a cookie.

## Optional generation and billing features

If you use the separate image or video generation workflow, the prompts, reference files, generation settings, and outputs you submit may be sent to the configured model and storage providers and saved with your task or account records. Paid features may store order, subscription, and credit records; payment details are processed by the selected payment provider. These optional workflows are separate from the public calculator and prompt-writing tool.

## Optional price alerts

The calculator has a separate, unchecked opt-in for price-update emails. Submitting it stores your email address, the consent wording version, and the time of consent. It does not create an account. Requests are collected for price-update notifications; delivery timing is not guaranteed. To withdraw a request or have your address removed, email the privacy contact below from the subscribed address. Registration alone does not subscribe you to these emails.

## Hosting and other services

Hosting and authentication infrastructure process technical information such as IP addresses, request logs, and session metadata to deliver the site and prevent abuse. If analytics or support integrations are enabled, those services may also process usage or support information. Account-only features may store information you explicitly submit through them. We do not sell the email addresses collected through the price-alert form.

## Retention and your choices

Account records are kept while needed to provide and secure your account. Alert requests are kept until you withdraw them or the notification service is discontinued. Technical logs and backups follow the hosting provider’s retention settings; we do not promise immediate erasure from backups. You can request access, correction, or deletion through the contact below. We may ask you to verify ownership before changing or removing records.
